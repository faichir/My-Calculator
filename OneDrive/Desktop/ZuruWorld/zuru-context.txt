Zuru World is a platform that connects people who need eyes on a physical place they cannot be (Seers) with verified local people already there with a smartphone (Showers).
Two products: (1) Verification Layer: live, directed, GPS-verified video sessions with two-way audio and a permanent timestamped archive. Uses: property viewing for diaspora and buyers, insurance claim field checks, supplier/warehouse verification for logistics, farm and project checks, NGO donor verification of funded projects, journalist verification. (2) World Experience Layer: live guided virtual tours of places and daily life.
Payment is per session through mobile money. Starting in East Africa (Nairobi). Every session is requested, paid and consented to by the person showing.
Target customers: insurance, real estate, logistics, NGOs and donors, media, diaspora services, tourism.
